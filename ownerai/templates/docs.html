Getting started

  1. Create the owner account on the first run - it becomes the owner of the installation.
  2. Sign in and open the dashboard.
  3. Register your cameras and give them a location label.
  4. Ask the assistant a question; it answers from your own camera metadata.

Privacy

  • Only metadata about events is stored. Raw frames and audio are not retained.
  • Biometric templates are encrypted with AES-GCM before they touch the database.
  • Templates expire automatically after the configured retention period and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Unenrolled people are never identified - they stay "Unknown".
  • Context that is sent to a third-party AI provider is redacted first.

Enrolling a face template

Open the dashboard, choose "Enrol an identity" and upload one or more pictures of yourself. The request must carry an explicit consent flag; without it the server refuses to store anything. Withdrawing consent deletes the template immediately.

Multi-factor authentication

Administrators and owners should enable a TOTP authenticator. The secret is stored encrypted and the shared secret is only shown once. Backup codes are provided for the case that the device is lost.

Device tokens

Phones, wearables and IP cameras push events with a device token and an HMAC signature over the timestamp, a nonce and the body. Nonces can only be used once, which prevents replay attacks.

Assistant safety rules

  • The assistant only sees your own data - permissions are checked on the server.
  • Cameras metadata and your messages are wrapped as untrusted data, so injected instructions cannot change the rules.
  • Mutating actions are never executed by the model; they wait for your explicit confirmation.
  • Daily token and request budgets bound the cost of the assistant.
