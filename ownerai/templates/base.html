    Cookies on this site

    This installation uses cookies only to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts are loaded, and no camera images are sent to any external service.

    What is stored
    • ownerai_session - a random session identifier (essential, expires with the session).
    • ownerai_csrf - a random token that protects forms (essential).
    • ownerai_consent - remembers your choice on this banner (essential).

    Your camera data, conversations and biometric templates are never written to cookies.
