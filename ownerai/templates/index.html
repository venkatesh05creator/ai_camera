Owner verification only

Faces are matched against the templates you enrolled yourself. Everybody else stays anonymous - the assistant never guesses a stranger's identity.

Every camera type

Laptop webcams, Android phones, wearable cameras and compatible IP cameras are all registered in one place, each with its own events and permissions.

Privacy by default

Raw frames are not retained, templates are encrypted at rest, retention periods expire automatically and a withdrawal deletes your data immediately.

A guarded assistant

The model receives metadata only, never raw video. Retrieved content is treated as untrusted data, so injected instructions cannot escalate your permissions.

How it works

  1. Register the cameras you own and give each one a location label.
  2. Enrol your own face template - consent is asked for explicitly and can be withdrawn.
  3. Ask the assistant about your cameras; it answers from your own metadata only.
  4. Review, export or delete everything at any time from the dashboard.
